# Ilias concentration kernel

Apply this before any paid Weft call. Dilution is the failure mode. Win one
money page in the top 3. Do not publish a cluster of thin URLs.

You are not Ilias Ism. Do not impersonate him in external mail. Use this
decision order on the evidence.

## Inputs

- Target domain and the URL that can earn the traffic.
- Market and language. Never inherit a provider default (many SEO APIs default
  to Germany / `de`).
- First-party Google Search Console export when the user has one.
- At most three commercial seeds the business actually sells. Positioning copy
  is not a seed.

## Search Console

GSC is first-party. Weft does not sell it. Do not search Weft for
`searchconsole`.

Typical export columns:

| File | Columns |
|---|---|
| Queries.csv | Top queries, Clicks, Impressions, CTR, Position |
| Pages.csv | Top pages, Clicks, Impressions, CTR, Position |

Read in this order: impressions × commercial intent, then position.

- Brand and brand-typo queries at position 1–4 are not Blitz targets.
- Operator junk (`"exa search" -site:…`) is not a money query.
- A string with **zero impressions** is not a Blitz target.
- The money *page* is the URL with the most impressions (often `/`).

If GSC is brand-only, do not invent non-brand articles. Keep the homepage (or
that top URL). Ask for, or take from the user, **one to three commercial
seeds**. Buy demand or SERP only for those seeds.

## Blitz / wait / kill

| Call | When |
|---|---|
| **Blitz** | One query with demand, the page is already indexed, current position is roughly 5–20, and the business earns money if that query wins. Then 30 days of pressure on **that page only**. |
| **wait** | Brand-only GSC, no demand evidence, weak domain, or the page is not indexed. Improve the one money page. Do not spray new URLs. |
| **kill** | Zero demand, slogan-as-query, or a SERP you cannot win with one honest page. |

Nobody serious guarantees a ranking. Control the page, the links, and the
process.

## Best links only

One relevant, crawled, in-content link from a real site beats a volume package.
If the live Weft index has no complete backlink contract, say **unknown**. Do
not pay an incomplete “backlink check”.

## GEO

Citations in AI answers need citable facts, sources, and clear structure. Do
not require special AI-only markup. Do not promise inclusion.
